### R.1.TC1.04 Grid Family
This family of grids is generated using Pointwise, hex dominant BL followed by isotropic tets in off-body regions. This corresponds to the Pointwise version F2 HexIsoTet. [description](https://hlpw6.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/grids/RANS/R.1.TC1.04/HLPW6_TC1_Cadence_HexIsoTet_Family_F2.pdf)
